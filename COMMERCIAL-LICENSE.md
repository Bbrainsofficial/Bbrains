# BBrains Commercial License

**Version 1.0 — DRAFT**

This document describes the commercial licensing terms for institutions and
organizations that use BBrains in a paid capacity. It is a draft to be finalized
with legal counsel. This is **not** legal advice.

## 1. Dual-license model

The BBrains source code is published under the GNU Affero General Public License
v3 (`LICENSE`). Anyone may read, modify, and self-host the community edition,
but AGPL's "network use = distribution" clause means that modified network
deployments must also release their source code under AGPL.

Colleges and organizations that do not want the AGPL obligations apply for a
separate **BBrains Commercial License**, which grants additional rights.

## 2. What the commercial license grants

A paid commercial license typically grants the licensee the right to:

- Self-host BBrains without the obligation to open-source proprietary
  customizations or integrations under AGPL.
- Receive priority technical support.
- Access premium themes, UI packs, and optional modules.
- Receive custom development and integration services (optional add-on).

It does **not** grant ownership of the BBrains codebase or trademarks.

## 3. Managed cloud (SaaS)

Institutions that do not want to run any infrastructure subscribe to the
BBrains managed cloud service. The BBrains team handles hosting, maintenance,
backups, updates, and security under a monthly per-institution or
per-active-user agreement. Data is stored in isolated per-institution
namespaces.

## 4. License tiers

| Tier               | Who it's for                          | Terms                             |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Community Edition  | Individuals, non-commercial use, small institutions under the free threshold | AGPL-3.0, no charge |
| Self-Hosted License | Colleges self-hosting with proprietary customizations | Annual per-institution license |
| Managed Cloud      | Colleges wanting zero infrastructure  | Monthly subscription |

## 5. Contributor license agreement (CLA)

Because the codebase contains community contributions, **every external
contributor signs a CLA** granting BBrains the right to distribute and relicense
their contribution. This is what makes the commercial license legally clean. See
`CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## 6. Trademarks

"BBrains" and related logos are trademarks. A commercial license does not grant
permission to use these marks beyond identifying the software in normal use
(attribution).

## 7. Termination

A commercial license may be terminated for material breach or non-payment.
Upon termination, the licensee must stop using the commercial rights and may
continue using the software only under the terms of AGPL-3.0.

## 8. Warranty and liability

Commercial licenses include the disclaimers and limitations of liability set
out in the applicable master services agreement. Nothing in AGPL-3.0 limits
these commercial terms for the licensed portions.
